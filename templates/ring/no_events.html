Not seeing motion events? Check your Ring settings:

  1. In the Ring app, select your camera and open Motion Settings.
  2. Open Smart Alerts and check the motion types your camera records and reports.
  3. Make sure motion recording and alerts are enabled, including Record and Send Alert when those options are available.

This app can only check events created after your Ring camera was connected. Once a new event arrives, your room snapshot and cleanliness score will appear on the dashboard.
