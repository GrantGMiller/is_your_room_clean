Thanks to everyone who signed up for DidYouCleanYourRoom.com. This is a fun side project I started to help keep track of my kids' chores.

A little about me

I'm a dad with a lot of kids to keep track of, and I found it hard to keep all their chores in my head. I made this app to help. When a kid asks, “Can I go to so-and-so's house?” I can just ask, “Did you do your checklist?”

We have the Wall Display set up on our refrigerator, which has a touchscreen with a web browser built in. I can pull up the chore page and see who has done what. Chores are added automatically at the right times of day, and the kids can go through them one by one so they don't forget anything.

Try the Chore Manager

You can follow the guide to add family members and set up chores. If you don't see the Chores button at the top of the page, open My Account and enable the Chores feature.

Get started with chores

You can set up your own Wall Display by following the Wall Display guide, or send the Wall Display link to your kids' phones or other devices.
